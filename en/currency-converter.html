How is the conversion calculated?

The table shows the CBI buying rate and the final selling-rate column for each listed currency against the Iraqi dinar. Cross-currency conversions use each currency’s IQD rate and the selected rate side. For currencies not listed in the CBI bulletin, a global indicative reference rate is used and is not presented as an official buying or selling quote.

Currency converter FAQs

Where do the exchange rates come from?

The primary source is the currency-rate bulletin published on the Central Bank of Iraq website. If a currency is not included, we use an indicative reference rate from ExchangeRate.dev and identify that source.

What is the difference between buying and selling rates?

The buying rate is taken from the CBI bulletin’s buying column. The selling rate used here is taken from the final selling column. The bulletin date is displayed beside the calculator.

Are the rates binding or real-time?

The CBI states that its bulletin rates are indicative and do not constitute an obligation on the Bank. Data is checked automatically when the source publishes an update; a rate cannot update before its source does.

Notice: CBI bulletin rates are indicative under the bulletin’s own terms. Rates for currencies not listed by the CBI are global references, not official buying or selling rates. Calculator results are not a binding offer to exchange currency.

Indicative reference rates for currencies not listed by the CBI are provided by ExchangeRate.dev.
